Privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form on this site: your name, work email, project details and any message. It is emailed to us at hello@stayon.site and used solely to reply to you and discuss your project.

Cookies and storage

We set no cookies. Your light/dark choice is kept in your browser's local storage and never sent to us.

Hosting and logs

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (such as IP address) to deliver and secure it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete anything you sent us by emailing hello@stayon.site. We keep form submissions only as long as needed to handle your enquiry.
